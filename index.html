Pix Relay: Auto Photo Sync

Automatically transfer photos and videos from your daily phone to your Backup Pixel over local Wi-Fi — encrypted end-to-end, with zero cloud middleman and zero mobile data usage.

How to Install on Your Phone

  1. Tap Download Latest APK above and select PixRelay-v1.0.0.apk under Assets.
  2. Open the downloaded APK from your browser downloads or Files app.
  3. If prompted, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source, then tap Install.
  4. Open Pix Relay on your Backup Pixel (choose Receiver) and on your daily phone (choose Sender), connect both to the same Wi-Fi, and scan the QR code in the Pairing tab.
