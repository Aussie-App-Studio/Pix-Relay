Privacy Policy — Pix Relay: Auto Photo Sync

Effective Date: November 24, 2026  ·  Publisher: Aussie App Studio  ·  Package: com.pixrelay.sync
The Short Version: Pix Relay: Auto Photo Sync does not collect, track, sell, or upload any of your personal data to Aussie App Studio or third-party analytics or ad servers. Your photos, videos, pairing keys, and transfer logs stay on your own devices and travel directly over your local Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot network.

1. Zero Developer Data Collection & No Telemetry

Aussie App Studio operates no backend servers for Pix Relay and does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share:

  • Personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or account credentials)
  • Photos, videos, albums, EXIF metadata, or GPS coordinates
  • Usage analytics, behavioral tracking, advertising identifiers, or crash reports

2. Local Wi-Fi & Hotspot Transfers Only

Pix Relay transfers photos and videos directly between your paired devices (for example, your daily phone and your backup Pixel, or the Pix Relay PC/Mac companion app):

  • Local Discovery: Uses local LAN UDP broadcast (UDP 42889) and Android Network Service Discovery / mDNS (_pixrelay._tcp.).
  • End-to-End Local Encryption: Control messages and media streams are sent over a direct local TCP connection (TCP 42888) encrypted with P-256 ECDH key exchange, HKDF-SHA256 key derivation, and AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption, verified with a QR pairing token or 6-digit code.
  • No Mobile Data Transfers: Cellular interfaces are excluded from media transfers so syncing only uses your local Wi-Fi or hotspot.

3. Android Permissions We Request & Why

  • Photos & Videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to display your media folders on a Sender phone, read selected photos/videos to transfer, and save received media onto a Receiver Pixel.
  • Media Location (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION): Used on Android 10+ solely so original EXIF GPS coordinates embedded inside your photos are preserved during local transfer. Pix Relay never reads or tracks your live device location.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Optional permission used only when you choose to scan a QR pairing code on another device. Camera frames are processed locally in real time and are never saved or transmitted.
  • Local Network & Wi-Fi (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE): Used for local LAN/hotspot device discovery and encrypted peer-to-peer socket transfers.
  • Background Operation & Notifications (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE, WAKE_LOCK, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS, POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Keeps Receiver mode ready and lets Auto-Send finish reliably while the screen is off, resumes after device restart if configured, and shows local transfer/battery/storage notifications.

4. Optional User-Configured Features & Third-Party Services

  • Google Photos (on Receiver Devices): Pix Relay writes received photos and videos to your Pixel's local storage (such as DCIM/Camera or Pictures/PixRelay/). Any cloud backup is handled exclusively by the Google Photos app on your device under your Google account settings and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Optional Smart Plug Battery Guard Webhooks: On a Receiver Pixel, if you explicitly enable Smart Plug Battery Guard and provide custom webhook URLs, Pix Relay sends an HTTP request solely to the URLs you entered when battery charge crosses your configured thresholds (e.g., 80% off / 20% on). Disabled by default.
  • External Companion Links: Tapping external PC/Mac companion links inside the app opens your device's web browser directly.

5. Local Storage, Retention & Deletion

Preferences (pix_relay_prefs) and the transfer ledger database (pix_relay.db — paired device keys, folder policies, and SHA-256/fingerprint deduplication history) are stored strictly locally on your device.

  • You can unpair any device inside the Pairing tab at any time to remove its shared secret.
  • You can uninstall Pix Relay or clear app storage in Android Settings to permanently delete all local app data.
  • If Android system backup is enabled on your device, Android may include app preferences in your personal device backup controlled by your Android account settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Pix Relay does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), because the app collects no personal data at all.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version and updated effective date will be published on this page.
