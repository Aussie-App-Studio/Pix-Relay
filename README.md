# Pix Relay — Auto Photo Sync (Android)

Automatically back up photos and videos from your daily phone to your Pixel over local Wi-Fi — **zero cloud middleman, zero mobile data, and encrypted end-to-end on your home network**.

## How to Download & Install Pix Relay on Your Phone

### Step 1: Download the APK
1. On your Android phone, open the **[Latest Release](https://github.com/Aussie-App-Studio/Pix-Relay/releases/latest)**.
2. Scroll down to **Assets** and tap **`PixRelay-v1.0.0.apk`** to download it.

### Step 2: Allow & Install
1. Tap the downloaded **`PixRelay-v1.0.0.apk`** file (in your browser notification or the **Files** app).
2. If Android warns *"For your security, your phone currently isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*:
   - Tap **Settings**
   - Turn ON **Allow from this source**
   - Go back and tap **Install**
3. Tap **Open** once installation finishes.

*(Note: If Google Play Protect asks to scan the app, tap **More details -> Install anyway** or wait for the quick scan to complete.)*

## Quick 2-Minute Setup

You need Pix Relay installed on **both** phones (your **Daily Phone** and your **Backup Pixel**) on the **same Wi-Fi network** (or mobile hotspot).

### 1. Set Up Your Backup Pixel (Receiver)
1. Open **Pix Relay** on your Backup Pixel and choose **Backup Pixel (Receiver)**.
2. Allow **Photos & Videos**, **Notifications**, and **Unrestricted Battery** when prompted so it can receive photos reliably even when the screen is off.
3. Keep the Pixel plugged in and connected to Wi-Fi, with **Google Photos** backup turned on for the Camera folder.

### 2. Set Up Your Daily Phone (Sender)
1. Open **Pix Relay** on your daily phone and choose **Daily Phone (Sender)**.
2. Allow **Photos & Videos** access (and **Camera** if you want to scan the pairing QR code).
3. Open the **Pairing** tab:
   - Tap **Scan QR Code** and point your camera at the QR code shown on your Backup Pixel, **or**
   - Tap your Backup Pixel under **Nearby Devices** and confirm the matching **6-digit code** on both screens.
4. Go to **Auto-Send**, turn it on, and make sure your **Camera** folder is set to **Auto**.

That's it! New photos and videos will automatically relay to your Backup Pixel over local Wi-Fi.
